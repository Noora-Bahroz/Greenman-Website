Product Definition

Designed to fit into the inner slot of a strut channel, where it provides a secure threaded anchor point for bolts and threaded rods. The top surface has serrations or grooves that grip the inner edges of the strut channel. These serrations prevent slipping and ensure a tight, stable fit. The channel nut is available in various variations to suit different applications. In the basic use, GPTNxx is combined with a nut, the combo is equipped with a spring that retains.
