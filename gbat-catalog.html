Product Definition

GBUH is used in framing, and support systems to ensure strut channels are firmly anchored to structural members. It secures the strut channel, distributing load evenly and ensuring a stable connection.

• per anchorage point two holders should be installed
• application loads refer to one single GBUHxxxx holder
• application loads of GM-profile should be considered
